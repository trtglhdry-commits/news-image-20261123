LEAD 1 • CLIMATE

COP31 ends with no deal

The UN climate summit in Antalya, Turkey closes without agreement — the longest summit on record and the first failure in 17 years. The EU and 90+ nations rejected a draft silent on fossil fuels.

LEAD 2 • UKRAINE

Sea drones sink a warship 1,500 miles out

Ukraine says 7 naval drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi, Libya, and hit a cargo ship. President Zelensky confirmed the strike — about 1,500 miles from the front line.

LEAD 3 • US–IRAN

Missiles, mines and a blockade

Trump says Iran launched its heaviest week of missiles and drones in over a year — all shot down, he says. Iran says it hit US Patriot radars in Bahrain, vows to use "all means" against the US naval blockade, and says 3 tankers caught fire after hitting mines in the Strait of Hormuz.
